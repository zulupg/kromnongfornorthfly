Candidate Biography

Dedicated Leadership for North Fly

Council Herman Kromong has served North Fly with unwavering dedication, advocating for community rights, economic growth, and accessible public services. His vision brings together local leaders, youth, and families to build a prosperous constituency.

With the campaign motto "Let's Change North Fly", Council Kromong is committed to delivering vital infrastructure, empowering local businesses, revitalizing healthcare facilities, and opening educational opportunities across all LLG areas.

Policy Manifesto

Four Policy Pillars of Change

Click any pillar below to explore Council Herman Kromong's priorities.

• Upgrading primary feeder roads connecting remote agricultural communities to market hubs.

• Solar-powered river transport stations and boat safety infrastructure along river channels.

• Telecoms and mobile network expansion to unserved rural wards.

• Fully stocked medical supply chains for all rural aid posts and health centers.

• Emergency medical evacuation support and mobile health patrol teams.

• Solar refrigeration for vaccines and life-saving medications in rural clinics.

• North Fly Education Bursary Fund supporting university and college students.

• Establishment of technical vocational training centers for carpentering, mechanics, and IT.

• Youth leadership initiatives and community sports facility developments.

• Micro-finance grants for women-led SMEs and local agricultural co-operatives.

• Cocoa, rubber, and agriculture seedling subsidies with direct access to markets.

• Modernized community market structures and financial literacy programs.
